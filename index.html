About

Method before medium.

I'm a psychopedagogue and instructional designer based in Brazil. Nearly a decade evaluating large language models for platforms like Leapforce, Appen, and Teamwork gave me technical intuition that most designers don't have — before AI evaluation became an industry unto itself.

My foundation is in psychopedagogy and epistemology. What that means in practice: I ask what it means to know something before I decide how to teach it. I build tools that treat learners as thinking agents.

The theory comes first. The design follows.

Workplace Conflict
Resolution Simulator

No branching trees, no multiple-choice. Just conversation.

A role-play simulator for navigating four distinct workplace conflict archetypes. Characters respond to how you communicate — empathy opens them up, blame closes them down. Powered by character-specific AI prompts with consistent psychological profiles.

Built in Vanilla JS by design: no framework, no dependencies, no build step. Anyone can open the repository and read exactly what is happening.

AI Coach · B2B Sales Training

Selly — AI Sales Coach

Deliberate practice. Without the social overhead.

A conversational coach for B2B sales reps. Users pick their struggle areas, choose how much scaffolding they want, and Selly runs the session from there — pushing back when answers are vague, quoting the rep's own words in feedback because abstract feedback is easy to ignore.

The design started with a 34-page knowledge base built before writing a line of the system prompt. The complexity in this project was instructional.
